Dumoji is a tiny iOS game about unexplained emoji situations and questionable
decisions. Every mystery asks you to discover a hidden relationship, sequence,
or rule among the emojis—and rewards a good guess with a joke.

The game includes **350 authored mysteries across 15 chapters**, with eleven
different puzzle systems: visual anomalies, chronology, memory matches, grids,
analogies, seating charts, nonograms, and more. It supports English and Turkish,
runs on iPhone and iPad, and keeps campaign progress on the device.

The first chapters are free. One optional, non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks
the rest of the campaign. There are no ads, subscriptions, accounts, or paid
hints.
