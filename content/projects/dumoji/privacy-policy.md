**Effective date: August 1, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Eyup Aydin ("I", "me", or "the developer")
handles information in **Dumoji**, the iOS game (the "App").

## The short version

Dumoji does not require an account and does not send gameplay data to a server.
It has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or social login. Game
progress and settings remain on your device.

## Information Dumoji does not collect

Dumoji does not collect or transmit your name, email address, contacts, photos,
precise location, microphone recordings, camera images, advertising identifier,
or gameplay analytics. The motion sensor is used locally for the sleeper
interaction; motion readings are not sent to the developer.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay progress, settings, and purchase state locally on your
device. The App does not currently synchronize this information through iCloud
or another cloud service. Removing the App may remove this local data, subject
to any device backup or operating-system behavior that Apple controls.

## In-app purchases

Dumoji offers one optional, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks the full
campaign. Purchases, payment processing, refunds, and purchase restoration are
handled through Apple and the App Store using StoreKit. I do not receive your
payment-card details. Apple may process information under [Apple's Privacy
Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and its applicable media-services
terms.

The App uses the purchase entitlement needed to unlock and restore the content;
it does not operate a payment account or maintain a purchase database on my
server.

## Children

Dumoji does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you
believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact me
at [ea@eaydin.net](mailto:ea@eaydin.net).

## Changes and contact

I may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The updated
version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For privacy
questions, contact [ea@eaydin.net](mailto:ea@eaydin.net).

This page describes the current Dumoji implementation. It is not a promise that
Apple's own services collect no information; Apple's policies govern those
services separately.
