---
title: "Threadling"
description: "A small macOS companion that holds the context for the task you need to return to."
weight: 1
---

Threadling is a little creature that sits on your screen and carries the context
of your current task in its backpack.

Press **Command + Shift + Space** to hand your current task to Threadling. Set a
timer for it to bring you back when you are ready, or open the backpack yourself
to see what you set aside and continue where you left off.
