Quick help

A few useful clues.

I bought the full game

Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase, then use Dumoji’s Restore Purchases action. If the full campaign is still locked, email me with your device model and iOS version.

My progress disappeared

Dumoji stores progress locally on your device and does not currently sync it through a server or iCloud. Deleting the app may remove local progress.

I found a puzzle problem

Send the chapter, puzzle number, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot is helpful. Please leave out passwords and other sensitive information.
